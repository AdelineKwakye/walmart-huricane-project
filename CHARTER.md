# 4. How We Work

### a. Where do we talk, and how fast do we answer?

- We will talk when needed over text, on a certain day during our meetings, and in person when we see each other for class. We will answer texts within the same day, or if we can’t answer immediately, we will communicate that with one another and be understanding.

### b. When do we meet outside class?

- We will meet weekly on Sundays at 6:30 pm, and at any other times agreed upon by both members, as needed.

### c. How do we decide when we disagree?

- When we disagree, we will review the data first. If disagreements are still there after looking at the data, we will let the owner of that part make the final decision. If it is a large disagreement that we feel needs outside assistance, we will email the professor or TA.

### d. What do we do if someone goes quiet?

- If someone goes quiet, we do not immediately assume they are ghosting the group. We will allow for at least a day for the person to get back to the group. If it is a more pressing issue, less time will be given for a response (based on how pressing the issue is). If the issue is not addressed, the professor will be contacted. We have another class together that meets on days we do not have data science systems, making going quiet less of an issue for us.
